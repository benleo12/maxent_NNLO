Protocol addendum, 2026-11-13, before production or any portability fit

The installed Sherpa build lacks LHAPDF support. Use Herwig 7.3.0 as the
independent-generator test, with the same analysis and validation definitions
already frozen in PROTOCOL.txt. This substitution follows a technical pilot,
without examining posterior agreement with any validation distribution.

Generate pp -> gamma*/Z -> e+e- at 13 TeV with MEqq2gZ2ff, mass 50--2200 GeV,
and no lepton generation cuts. Use CT18NNLO for the hard-process PDF and the
generator's standard angular-ordered QCD shower, including its default
hard-emission matrix-element correction. Keep hadronization and MPI with the
default tune and its shower/MPI PDFs. Disable QED shower radiation and retain
the empty default post-subprocess-handler list. Record the complete generator
settings and integration statistics. This compares complete working priors,
not an isolated shower change at identical tune parameters.

The independent 1000-event technical pilot returned 365 selected events with
unit positive nominal weights, valid prompt electron ancestry and physical
kinematics. No posterior was fitted to the pilot. Produce 1.5 million events
in independent seeds, targeting at least 500000 selected events. Extend only
if required by the predeclared event-count/statistical-precision criterion.

Use a bounded-memory HepMC reader with the public adapter. Preserve compact
arrays, event number plus shard identity, nominal weights, four-vectors and
all generated/selected counts and weight sums. Keep a small unmodified full
HepMC subset for record round-trip checks. Full production records are streamed
and not archived. This explicitly supersedes the full-record retention sentence
in PROTOCOL.txt, since the pilot is 157 MB per 1000 events.

Compare shapes after unit-area normalization, apply exactly the released DY
fixed-order moments and event selection, and report selected orders and
constrained closure. The angular reference is independent of moment training.
Do not tune moments, maps, settings or cuts against validation agreement.

Public subset rule, fixed before production finishes or any portability fit:
use seed731131 and the same strata/caps as the released Pythia fixture,
[0,30,60,100,200,500,infinity) GeV with caps[50000,20000,20000,15000,12000,12000].
Uniform sampling without replacement within each stratum; retain all events
where the population is below the cap. Divide original weights by the inclusion
probability. Preserve unique IDs, shard/native IDs and lepton four-vectors.
The manuscript comparison uses the full priors; public subsets are separately
identified demonstrations with their own sampling statistics.
