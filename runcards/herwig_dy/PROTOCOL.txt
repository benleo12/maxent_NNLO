Frozen study: reuse of the released Drell--Yan moments across generators
Date: 2026-11-13

Purpose: apply unchanged published fixed-order inputs and solver prescriptions
to an independently generated, positive-weight Sherpa prior, alongside the
existing MG5+Pythia prior. This is a portability study, not an isolation of a
single shower parameter and not a claim of generator-independent unconstrained
accuracy.

Sherpa 3.0.4, pp at 13 TeV, e+e- with zero/one additional parton matrix elements
merged at 20 GeV. CT18NNLO central PDF. No lepton generation cuts, generation
mass 50--2200 GeV. QED corrections to leptons disabled. Shower, fragmentation
and underlying event retained. Record every explicit setting and the complete
Sherpa settings report, version and integration/generation logs.

Analysis selection matches released dy exactly: 66<mll<116 GeV, both leptons
pT>27 GeV and |eta|<2.5, pre-QED-radiation lepton definition. Original event
weights and event identifiers are preserved. Compare normalized predictions
to separate shape from differences in the Born normalization.

Use load_moments('dy') and upgrade without changing moment maps or orders to
improve agreement in validation. The released order-selection prescription is
applied to each prior; report selected orders. Repeat at fixed released-Pythia
orders as a diagnostic if defaults select different degrees. Keep fixed-order
and prior uncertainties distinguishable.

Display constrained mll, |yll| and qT and withheld |cos(theta_CS)|, lepton pT,
and dilepton acoplanarity. Use existing fixed-order reference predictions
where available with compatible accuracy/phase space. No calibration to the
withheld distributions. Report global and analysis-bin effective counts,
largest weight contributions, fit closure and measured runtime.

Pilot: 1000 generated events to verify settings, positive weights, HepMC record
validity and extraction. Production target: at least 500000 selected events,
with independent random seeds. Extend only to improve statistical precision,
never to tune agreement. Avoid claims from sparsely populated tail bins.

Public demonstration: deterministic subset of real events, independent
reference histograms, generation/extraction recipe, weights and uncertainty
workflow. Preserve a real HepMC subset for full event-record round trips.
Full production records remain on the cluster with checksums/provenance.
