Two-jet refinement at fixed statistics
====================================

This follow-up uses the same 16,004 prior events and fixed-order inputs as the
original Z+2-jet benchmark. The original reference and coarse-grid results were
already known. The new merging rule and scan were specified before computing
the refined fits, without tuning to their outcomes. See PROTOCOL.txt, including
the explicitly identified supplemental uncertainty diagnostic.

Histogram starting grids have 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 or 10 logarithmic bins per momentum.
Adjacent sparse cells are merged using only prior counts and geometry until
each group contains at least 25 original events. This gives 2, 4, 6, 8, 16 and
28 independent shape constraints. Groupings stay fixed in all resamples.
All moment and merged-histogram fits succeed in all 256 paired resamples.

The common comparison cells contain >=50 prior events and 97% of reference
probability. The binned distance uses probabilities normalized on the entire
hard region; it does not renormalize inside these cells. The correction-spread
diagnostic DOES normalize the learned correction shape within these cells.

Representative results (percent):
                         constraints   distance    correction RMS, common cells
Separate moments              8          1.72                   3.14
Joint moments, degree 2      12          1.47                   3.40
Merged histogram, B=5         8          4.39                   2.43
Merged histogram, B=7        16          2.93                   3.29
Merged histogram, B=10       28          1.04                   4.38

At eight constraints, moments describe the joint distribution more closely.
Finer histogram corrections are competitive in two dimensions. Their correction
fluctuations increase as they gain resolution; adding higher joint-moment
degrees also increases fluctuations. This supports a compact representation,
not universal statistical superiority of moments or monotonic growth of errors
on every physical prediction. Fitted bin probabilities are constrained, so
their uncertainty differs from uncertainty of the ratio used to correct them.

Numerical interpretation
The correction is q/p. Each resampled fit is evaluated at fixed original event
locations, normalized in the stated region, and divided by the central
correction. Its pointwise standard deviation is squared, averaged with the
central posterior probabilities in that region, and square-rooted. This is a
shape diagnostic, not a cross-section uncertainty or a theory uncertainty.

The originally specified FULL-region correction RMS is also retained. For
moment fits it is dominated by extrapolation to an extreme event omitted by
some Poisson resamples: pTj1=856.7, pTj2=660.2 GeV, in a comparison cell with one
prior event. It is about 573% for the degree-two joint fit, versus 3.40% within
the fixed populated region. This is not the statistical error on its accepted
cross section. It demonstrates that agreement in the populated region does not
validate extrapolation into sparse tails. See the HTML table and summary.json.
The common-region shape diagnostic was added after identifying this effect;
both diagnostics are reported, and no model or comparison-cell mask was changed.

Files and reproduction
summary.json records every model, original grid counts and probabilities,
merged groups, numerical spreads, cut predictions, effective counts, failures
and hashes. aggregate.npz contains predictions and 256 paired resamples, plus
independently resampled reference values. It contains no event kinematics.
PROTOCOL_original.txt preserves the initial protocol; PROTOCOL.txt records its
diagnostic addendum. The original target/reference inputs are in ../zj_benchmark.

Run from the repository root:
  python examples/plot_zj_refinement.py --output zj_refinement_plots
  python examples/zj_refinement.py full_compatible_prior.npz refinement_run
  python examples/plot_zj_refinement.py --results refinement_run --output new_plots

Use a compute host for full-event fitting. The public compact Z+jet fixture is
not the 16,004-event benchmark prior and will not reproduce these numbers.
The study does not add fixed-order accuracy or validate a general claim for
arbitrarily many observables. In higher dimensions, histogram cells scale as
b^d; the gain from moments requires a physically adequate truncated basis.
