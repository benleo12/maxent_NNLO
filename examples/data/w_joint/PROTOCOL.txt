Frozen W prediction study, 2026-11-13

Purpose
Test whether production/decay constraints selected without inspecting lepton
validation distributions improve predictions for those distributions and for
new fiducial cuts. Retain negative or inconclusive outcomes.

Physics and events
W+ and W- at 7 TeV. Baseline pT_l>20 GeV, |eta_l|<2.5, pT_nu>25 GeV,
mT>40 GeV. NNLOJET input settings and electroweak scheme remain those of the
released W_MOMENTS calculations: CT18NNLO central PDF, seven stated scale
choices with mu=m_lnu, CMS/Gmu, M_W=80.385 and Gamma_W=2.085 on-shell
parameters, M_Z=91.1876, Gamma_Z=2.4952, full CKM. This study will document
the existing prior's distinct electroweak parameters rather than silently alter
them. No new event generation is necessary for this experiment.

Use the current positive-weight no-MEC priors on Mac mini. The decay variable
is the SIGNED Collins-Soper angle of the charged lepton, with its z axis
folded along the boson longitudinal momentum exactly as in the released
moment code. The rapidity variable is |y_W| mapped on [0,4].

Frozen model hierarchy
P: prior, normalized to the NNLO baseline rate for shape comparisons.
B: mass, |y_W| and recoil constraints of the released process.
A: B plus coherent windowed angular constraints w(c)T_n(c), n=0..6.
J2: A plus the complete 0<=k,n<=2 rapidity-angle block, adding only functions
not already in A: w(c)T_k(u_y)T_n(c), k=1..2, n=0..2.
J4: analogous complete block through degree four, k=1..4, n=0..4.
The angle window is the existing smooth profile in c+1, with endpoints
(0.3,0.4,1.6,1.7), hence one for |c|<=0.6 and zero for |c|>=0.7.
Unlike the paper's blended angular target, A and J use identical windowed
definitions and direct fixed-order sums. The current blended-angle fit will
be retained as a separately labeled anchor, not confused with A.

Constraint selection
Central single-observable degrees are frozen from training only and held
fixed for every variation. The available J2/J4 blocks are fixed now, not
chosen from validation plots. Training residuals and their correlated
statistical covariance determine whether the extra block is resolved. Scale
variations are evaluated coherently as whole vectors, never as independent
Gaussian errors. Report the residual block statistic and all seven scale
vectors. Select the smallest complete candidate block that removes resolved
training residuals in the remaining degree-four block, subject to numerical
feasibility and statistical conditioning. If no block is resolved, retain A.
No validation observable can trigger an extra constraint or degree change.

Implementation of the training statistic, specified before production results:
Use the covariance of (predicted diagnostic moments - their fixed-order sums),
including correlations induced by the fitted constraints, via the derivative
Cov_q(H,Phi) [Cov_q(Phi,Phi)+ridge]^{-1}. Add independent finite-prior covariance,
including the prior contribution to blended recoil targets. Whiten the residual
correlation matrix and retain eigenmodes above 1e-6 of its largest eigenvalue.
Use a chi-square diagnostic with the retained rank. A block is called resolved
only if p<0.01 for ALL seven coherent scale residual vectors (evaluated with
the central statistical covariance); scales are not random Gaussian draws.
A tests the entire degree-four block; J2 tests its remaining 14 functions; J4
is the last predefined complete candidate. Select A, then J2, then J4 at the
first block without a resolved residual. Retain results for all candidates.
This is an approximate finite-Monte-Carlo diagnostic, not a calibrated coverage
claim. The fixed ridge is the released solver default 1e-4. Report convergence,
ridge residuals relative to input statistics, and effective statistics explicitly.
If any frozen candidate's supplied target misses by more than one input Monte
Carlo error at any coherent scale, flag it as failing the target-closure check.
Do not conceal that failure or tune the ridge after reading validation.

Independent integrations
Reuse already-adapted grids for identical phase space, not previous production
events. Use disjoint seeds for training and validation, with disjoint channel
and charge offsets. Every group includes LO,R,V,RR,RV,VV and all seven scale
choices. All production histograms are archived, but validation predictions
remain unread until model choices and central weights are frozen.
Initial production: 32 independent runs/group/channel/charge, each 250000
points. Pilot: 20000 points/channel/charge for runtime, compatibility and
numerical sanity checks only. Extend statistically, without choosing models
from validation shapes, if the independent fixed-order precision is inadequate.
Target precision: relative error <=0.3% for nested-cut acceptances and <=1%
for populated charged-lepton eta bins; asymmetry absolute error <=0.002.
Use per-run scatter and channel independence for covariance, with ratio and
asymmetry uncertainties computed from coherent run resampling. Document
unresolved bins rather than silently merge them after seeing discrepancies.

Held-out predictions
At pT_l thresholds 20,27,30 GeV, with other baseline cuts unchanged:
separate-charge absolute |eta_l| spectra on the existing ATLAS edges
[0,.21,.42,.63,.84,1.05,1.37,1.52,1.74,1.95,2.18,2.5], their charge asymmetry,
total rates, and each new-cut acceptance relative to the baseline rate.
Also validate baseline pT_l and mT distributions on declared runcard bins.
None of these measurements is used in the weight fits.

Inference
Keep the same event weights when changing cuts. Compare B,A,selected J and
the prior, including event statistics and independent fixed-order statistics.
Input-statistical and coherent scale variations are retained separately.
Success means improvement in independent, unfitted distributions and new-cut
rates, beyond noise, with acceptable effective statistics. Failure means this
constraint set is insufficient, not that the held-out results may be fitted
retroactively. The experiment establishes performance only for the tested
observables/cuts; it cannot establish universal NNLO+PS or uncertainty coverage.

Numerical implementation, fixed before production: book the recoil polynomials
directly in log(pT) on [25,500], the released solver's acting range. This is
the same polynomial span as the booked [25,2500] basis, without an ill-conditioned
degree-20 affine transformation of rounded text accumulators. The window and
degree-selection rule are unchanged. Original pilot accumulators are retained.
