Z+2-jet held-out benchmark
=========================

This directory is an isolated, reproducible study. It does not change the
manuscript, the released package, or the shared NNLOJET build.

Scientific scope
---------------
All methods correct the same existing shower prior inside the hard region
60 <= pTj2 <= pTj1 < 1000 GeV. Each distribution is normalized to unit area
there. The reference is the R contribution to NLO Z+jet, namely LO Z+2jet
with these two resolved jets. This is a controlled shape comparison. It is
not an NNLO+PS proof or a validation of the transition prescription.

The earlier Figure 8 supplies independent coarse-grid closure for one joint
fit. Here the train and test integrations are new and independent. The study
adds ordinary histogram comparators at the same integration budget, a frozen
basis/resolution sequence, and withheld asymmetric cut efficiencies and jet
balance distributions. It retains numerical covariance and local event support.
The angular distribution is a diagnostic of absent angular information.

The benchmark uses the public upgrade API, including fixed orders and L2=1e-4.
Ordinary histogram IPF alternates exact marginal ratios; the direct histogram
ratio imposes the joint cell probabilities. No smoothing or clipping is added.
An empty prior cell with positive target mass is reported as failure, with the
missing probability stated explicitly. Such a failure can concern very little
mass and should not be presented as a large physics error.

Statistical conventions
-----------------------
32 paired replicas resample the 32 training runs and Poisson-resample prior
events. Every model uses the same resampling draws. Model orders stay fixed.
Fits normalize the ratio of aggregate numerator to aggregate denominator.
The independent validation covariance comes from a delete-one-run jackknife,
including conditional normalization. It is kept separate from fitting noise.
Numerical pulls combine these two independent numerical covariances. They are
not theory-exclusion significances. The deterministic scale curves are saved
separately. Failed replicas are counted, never silently replaced.

The two-dimensional TV on populated cells uses cells with at least 50 prior
events, without renormalizing the surviving cells. Its reported reference mass
specifies the fraction included. Full-grid TV and each sparse cell's count,
local effective count and maximum weight factor are also saved. Conditional
balance probabilities sum to one within each fixed leading-jet interval.

Artifacts
---------
training_fo.npz, validation_fo.npz: run-level cross sections, all seven scales.
integration_summary.json: point budgets, rates and integration timing.
benchmark_results.npz: all central/scaled predictions, paired statistical
replicas, validation covariance, moment gaps and local support diagnostics.
benchmark_summary.json: fixed models, solver diagnostics, failures and timing.
metrics.csv: all model accuracies, effective counts and cut predictions.
analysis.json: numerical errors, deterministic scale ranges and paired changes
as the joint block is enlarged. No test-driven selection is performed.
benchmark_overview.pdf/png: generated from aggregate caches by analyze.py.

To regenerate the tables/figure on the laptop, only aggregate caches are needed:
  /Users/user/miniconda3/envs/env_ba/bin/python analyze.py

Implementation notes
--------------------
The mini's Accelerate BLAS emits stale floating-point status warnings after
finite matrix multiplies. benchmark.py suppresses only those specific matmul
messages; positivity, normalization, finite values and solver convergence are
still checked. The pinned package source is unchanged.
