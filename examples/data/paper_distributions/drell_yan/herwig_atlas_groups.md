# Herwig lepton acoplanarity in the ATLAS measurement

The [table](herwig_atlas_groups.csv) gives the Herwig and ATLAS fiducial probabilities in four intervals of dilepton acoplanarity. Each measured probability is an exact sum of complete ATLAS bins. The prediction uses the full 572,823-event Herwig sample and the original fitted weights as recorded in its published acoplanarity histogram. [ATLAS Born-level measurement](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.92377.v3/t5)

The two bin grids share only the origin. To integrate a partial Herwig bin we assume a decreasing spectrum across that bin and its two neighbors. The three measured bin averages then bound the partial integral. Complete Herwig bins retain their published integrals. We use the midpoint of the lower and upper bounds for each scale member. The largest relative half-width in the four groups is 0.74%. This construction was checked against direct event integrals for all seven scale choices in the full MG5+Pythia sample and the compact Herwig sample. All 56 checks fall inside their brackets. The largest midpoint differences are 0.042% and 0.068% for the two checks.

The columns give the central and six scale-varied probabilities and both bounds for each variation. The event error includes the common fiducial normalization. The numerical input error comes from the spread of fits to resampled moments. The Herwig band in the ratio to data spans the scale variations and their partial-bin bounds. The experimental band uses the uncertainties in the original ATLAS bins. Each group contains several experimental bins, whose combined uncertainty requires their covariance.
