# Total Higgs recoil at 13 TeV

The data row compares the production samples in the paper with the ATLAS full-phase-space Higgs transverse-momentum spectrum. ATLAS combines the four-lepton and diphoton decay channels. The upper and middle panels show the gluon-fusion process. For the comparison with the measured total we add the same ATLAS prediction for VBF, VH, ttH, bbH and tH to each gluon-fusion sample. Throughout, the gluon-fusion rate is 48.58 pb. All 11 measured bins are in the archive. The figure shows the ten bins from 0 to 650 GeV. [ATLAS publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08615)

The input samples contain 2,000,000 gluon-fusion events before and after matching, 600,000 MiNNLOPS events and 1,000,000 MC@NLO events. The numerical table retains the generator signs. For each sample we first reproduce the central cross sections, the original event errors and the full scale envelope on the frozen figure bins. The largest relative difference between reproduced and frozen central values is below 4 × 10⁻¹³. We then sum the event weights directly in each ATLAS bin. The input file hashes and this check are in `inclusive_higgs_recoil.json`.

In `inclusive_higgs_recoil.npz` the edges are in GeV and the differential cross sections are in pb/GeV. `data_pb_per_GeV` is the ATLAS measurement and `xh_pb_per_GeV` is the ATLAS non-gluon-fusion contribution. Each `*_ggf_pb_per_GeV` array gives the gluon-fusion central values. The corresponding `*_total_pb_per_GeV` array adds XH. The first row of each `*_ggf_scale_pb_per_GeV` array is the central result. The remaining rows contain the complete event-weight scale variations. The fixed XH contribution uses the published ATLAS central value.

The plotted event errors include the statistical correlation between the bin and the sum of all generator weights. For bin fraction P, the variance is the sum of squared event weights inside the bin times (1−P)² plus the sum outside the bin times P², divided by the square of the total event weight. The archive provides these errors and the historical fixed-normalization errors separately. The plotted prediction bands combine the gluon-fusion shape-scale envelope and gluon-fusion normalization uncertainty in quadrature. The grey band divides the measured upper-panel experimental interval by the measured central value. The underlying PDFs do not resolve the experimental error-bar endpoints in the 120–200 and 200–300 GeV bins. Both central comparisons remain visible there, with the experimental band open. The separate wider grey band from the published ATLAS ratio panel is retained in the archive.

The [binned predictions and measurement](inclusive_higgs_recoil.npz) and [production validation](inclusive_higgs_recoil.json) give all numerical inputs. The [ATLAS source description](README.md) identifies the published central values and uncertainties.
