# ATLAS combined recoil in the full Higgs production phase space

ATLAS combined H→ZZ*→4ℓ and H→γγ at 13 TeV using 139 fb⁻¹. Its Figure 3a gives the measured cross section for inclusive pp→H+X and the central XH contribution from VBF, VH, ttH, bbH and tH. The black dashed step along the top of the hatched area is XH. It is the contribution to add to a gluon-fusion prediction before dividing by the measured inclusive cross section. The measurement is unfolded to full phase space. Its native edges are specified in Table 3. The measured experimental uncertainties are taken from the absolute spectrum. The log version of the same data and predictions in Auxiliary Figure 2 resolves the small high-recoil cross sections. [Publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08615) · [ATLAS figures and supporting material](https://atlas.web.cern.ch/Atlas/GROUPS/PHYSICS/PAPERS/HIGG-2022-04/index.php)

The native vectors are rounded to drawing coordinates. The combined central values in the linear and logarithmic panels agree within 0.84% up to 120 GeV. For XH, the two panels agree within 1.6% up to 300 GeV. The log panel retains relative precision into the tail. Its vector quantization corresponds to approximately 0.9% in a density. Integrated XH is 7.129 pb, consistent with the ATLAS sum of about 7.1 pb for the five additional modes. The integral of the data points is 55.80 pb, compared with the separately fitted inclusive measurement of 55.5 pb. No separate XH scale-variation curves accompany the dashed step.

The files keep the published grey ratio-panel limits separate from the data error-bar endpoints in the upper panel. The upper-panel endpoints are absolute densities. They are unresolved from underneath the markers at 120–200 and 200–300 GeV. Both lower-panel grey endpoints are clipped in the last 650–13000 GeV bin and its lower upper-panel endpoint reaches the log-axis frame. The published grey band is about √2 wider than the upper-panel fractional errors where both can be resolved. Our figure uses the measured absolute uncertainties. No covariance is available from these figures.

The [published linear spectrum](https://atlas.web.cern.ch/Atlas/GROUPS/PHYSICS/PAPERS/HIGG-2022-04/fig_03a.pdf) and [logarithmic spectrum](https://atlas.web.cern.ch/Atlas/GROUPS/PHYSICS/PAPERS/HIGG-2022-04/figaux_02.pdf) identify the original vector sources.
