# Higgs fixed-order comparisons

The arrays in `higgs_fixed_order.npz` contain the independent fiducial gluon-fusion calculation and the corrections used to compare it with particle-level predictions. The matching fit uses inclusive Higgs moments. None of the fiducial fixed-order histograms enters the fit. For jet categories with at least one jet the figure uses the published ATLAS comparison in `atlas_nnlojet_jets.json`.

The fiducial calculation uses NNLOJET HTO2P for gluon fusion in the heavy-top effective theory at 13 TeV. The two Higgs decay photons satisfy the ATLAS transverse-momentum and pseudorapidity cuts. Jets use anti-$k_T$ with $R=0.4$, $p_T>30$ GeV and $|y|<4.4$. Twenty independent production runs include LO, R, V, RR, RV and VV at seven renormalization and factorization scale choices. For each scale the differential cross sections are normalized to the same 48.58 pb inclusive gluon-fusion rate and a diphoton branching fraction of 0.00227. The rate under the native HTO2P settings is divided out before this normalization. The first leading-jet bin contains zero-jet events on the same convention as ATLAS.

The fixed-order jets are built from final-state partons. ATLAS uses stable particles, charged-particle isolation and jet–photon overlap removal. To relate the two definitions, we measure the ratio of particle-level to primary parton-shower yields on the same events in each displayed bin. Both records retain the photon kinematic cuts. The particle record applies the published ATLAS selection. The main correction uses eight independent 1,024-event Pythia streams. Eight independent 1,024-event MiNNLOPS samples test its dependence on the radiation model. The numerical error includes the paired sampling error and the independent NNLOJET integration error. The fixed-order band combines the scale envelope and the absolute shift between the two shower estimates in quadrature. Each scale also includes the same simulated VBF, WH, ZH and ttH contributions used for the shower predictions. [ATLAS fiducial definition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00487)

The recoil comparison starts at 80 GeV. The 60–80 GeV correction differed between the independent Pythia blocks by 2.65 combined sampling errors. The native 120–350 GeV leading-jet bin and 0.60–0.75 rapidity bin gave particle corrections differing between generators by over two combined sampling errors. The fixed-order comparison therefore combines complete neighboring bins into 90–350 GeV and 0.30–0.90, respectively. The wider hard-recoil bins also keep sampling errors under 5 percent. Every displayed transfer passes the half-sample stability test and the independent generator check. Both ratios use the same numerator and denominator edges within each comparison. The data panel keeps the native ATLAS bins.

For exclusive jet multiplicity the zero-jet reference uses HTO2P at NNLO. The other bins use the H+jet NNLOJET calculation and the additional Higgs modes shown in the ATLAS publication. This gives NNLO, NLO and LO for exactly one, exactly two and at least three jets. The last bin is outside the perturbative reach of inclusive HTO2P at NNLO. The ATLAS additional modes include VBF, VH, ttH, bbH and tH. The published central predictions for the three bins are 15.817, 7.340 and 3.589 fb. The vector coordinates of the absolute spectrum and its ratio to data reproduce one another within 0.0007 fb. The uncertainty band is copied from the published prediction. [ATLAS Figure 9a](https://atlas.web.cern.ch/Atlas/GROUPS/PHYSICS/PAPERS/HIGG-2019-13/fig_09a.pdf)

## Array fields

The observable prefixes are `jet`, `multiplicity`, `diphoton_pt` and `diphoton_absy`. Each observable has `edges`, `parton_members`, `parton_integration_error`, `particle_factor`, `particle_factor_mc`, `minnlo_factor`, `minnlo_factor_mc`, `other_modes`, `scale_members`, `numerical_error`, `nonperturbative_change` and `valid`. Member zero is central. The two `effective` arrays report the smaller effective count before and after the particle selection. The archive also retains the independently computed multijet HTO2P bins for comparison. Only its zero-jet bin is used in the jet-count figure. The alternative official reference for bins with jets is stored in `atlas_nnlojet_jets.json` under `absolute`, in the order central, lower, upper. Shape densities are in fb/GeV for transverse momentum and fb for absolute rapidity. Jet multiplicities are in fb.

The [fixed-order inputs](higgs_fixed_order.npz) and [published jet-bin reference](atlas_nnlojet_jets.json) contain the numerical denominators used in the figures.
