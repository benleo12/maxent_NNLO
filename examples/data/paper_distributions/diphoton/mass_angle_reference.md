# Continuum diphotons in the ATLAS mass and angular bins

The [comparison archive](mass_angle_reference.npz) contains both fitted probabilities, the independent NNLO central prediction and its scale bounds, and the ratios for all 26 diphoton mass bins and 25 pseudorapidity-angle bins. The mass spectrum covers 0–1700 GeV. The angular variable is the hyperbolic tangent of half the absolute difference in photon pseudorapidity. Each numerator is calculated with the original event weights of the full merged or shower-only sample. The model errors include the shared fiducial normalization at fixed fitted weights. The companion event and data archive gives the experimental errors in the same bins. [ATLAS measurement](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03839)

The fixed-order reference is the NNLOJET calculation of Gehrmann, Glover, Huss and Whitehead, with smooth-cone isolation, NNPDF3.1 and central scales equal to the diphoton mass. Its upper and lower scale bounds are the published pointwise envelope. Each bound is divided by the same central fiducial cross section as the central prediction. The central fiducial integral from the full angular range is 13.890 pb. The measured mass range contains 99.81% of this rate. The central values were recovered independently from the authors’ vector figures 14 and 16 and agree within 0.01%. [Independent NNLO calculation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.11310)

For `m` (mass) or `eta` (angle), `*_edges` gives the bin edges, `*_reference`, `*_lower` and `*_upper` give dimensionless bin probabilities, and `*_fiducial_fb` gives the central rate in fb. A density is the bin probability divided by its width. Each generator supplies the fitted probability, the ratio to the central reference and the finite-event error on that ratio.
