Old-cut population/migration interpretation
-------------------------------------------
For old30, selected low-qT (<25 GeV) migration residuals are
WP -0.1412 +/-0.0512 pp; WM -0.1346 +/-0.0482 pp.
In the 25--50 GeV region they are -0.0039 and +0.0130 pp; above50 they are
-0.0113 and -0.0235 pp. The remaining recoil-sensitive discrepancy is thus
concentrated at low recoil, while the hard-region migration is much improved.

The independent 3x3 joint map locates almost the entire old30 low-recoil
accepted-rate deficit outside the angular window, at |c|>0.7:
WP -0.1482 +/-0.0175 pp; WM -0.1871 +/-0.0143 pp.
The two other polar cells in that recoil slice are compatible with zero.
Near the W resonance, |c|>0.7 gives b=m*sqrt(1-c^2)/2 below about28.7 GeV,
so passing pTl>30 there requires recoil migration. The off-shell mass tail
prevents calling the whole cell strictly Born-forbidden.

Old27 remains a counterexample to uniform improvement. Its selected low-qT
migration residuals are +0.3149 +/-0.0401 pp and +0.2429 +/-0.0368 pp. At
25--50 GeV the remaining accepted-rate excess is mostly projected population,
not migration. The exact ledger separates these additive contributions but
does not uniquely establish missing resummation, a shower error, or the
optimal physical low-recoil distribution.
