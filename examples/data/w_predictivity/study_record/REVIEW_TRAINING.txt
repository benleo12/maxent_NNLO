tail probability using the full block dimension. Every addition/retention

All covariance matrices were exactly symmetric in the serialized records.
All standardized covariance eigenvalues exceeded the declared relative rank
cutoff. Ranks are D=4, M=6, H=15, S=15; there are no discarded-mode flags.

Both charges and both fixed training halves follow the declared single-pass
sequence: do not add D; do not add M; add H; add S. All select R+H+S. H and S
are far from the significance boundary at all seven coherent scales. The
all-scale condition is a deterministic scale-robust addition rule; scale
variations have not been treated as Gaussian statistical observations.

The half-prediction check uses the same prior and feature set and compares

The largest central differences divided by this expectation are 1.8292 (WP)

for large fit instability, not a new selection rule, a global p-value, or an
independent validation. Multiplier differences are archived; raw multiplier
sizes alone are not physically invariant stability measures.

Interpretation safeguards
------------------------
R changes the recoil target from the blended prescription to direct
windowed fixed-order moments. R-minus-anchor must be shown separately from
the effect of H/S. H turns on over 25--50 GeV and ends at 500 GeV. S is global,
including above 500 GeV; an H-to-H+S change cannot uniquely be called a
low-recoil effect. Both blocks vanish at Born level and their inclusive NNLO
targets contain NLO W+jet information. They supply no resummation. Fiducial
harmonic moments are measurement functions, not uncut angular coefficients.

The exact accepted-fraction ledger I=I0+(I-I0) can locate differences in the
projected population and recoil-sensitive migration, with a common original
baseline denominator for each model. It does not by itself establish a
unique shower or resummation cause. Improving agreement with a fixed-order
cut value is not proof of a more accurate shower in the low-recoil region.
