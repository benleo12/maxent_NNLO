Final precision allocation, fixed before any central validation comparison

The R/RV extension achieved the acceptance and individual-bin precision
criteria: relative acceptance errors below 0.1% and every eta bin below 1%.
The asymmetry errors were 0.0015-0.0038, so the declared 0.002 goal was not
met. A precision-only allocator used the full per-channel seed variance,
the asymmetry derivatives, and measured run times to distribute a final
independent sample. It never exposed central validation means or residuals.

Forecast target: maximum asymmetry MC error 0.0019, a 5% margin. Forecast
cost: 311.23 core-hours, or 18.24 ideal minutes on eight 128-core CPU nodes.
The final extension contains 10496 additional 250000-point runs. Long runs
are scheduled first to reduce idle tails. Final per-charge channel counts:
             LO      R      V      RR      RV      VV
  WP        864   2976     96    3296     768      32
  WM       1056   1760     64    1824     736      32
All original runs are retained with equal weight within each channel.

New seeds are 100000 + charge_index*100000 + channel_index*10000 + replica,
with WP/WM indices 0/1 and channel order LO,R,V,RR,RV,VV. Replica indices
start at that channel's previous count and end at the final count minus one.
These blocks are disjoint from all original, pilot and previous-extension
seeds, and lie below NNLOJET's 1000000 seed ceiling. A proposed larger seed
range was caught in a source audit and its queued job 61258800 was cancelled
before it ran. That rejected manifest is retained separately. Corrected
production is Perlmutter job 61258856.

Both fitted models and all event weights remain frozen: WP A, WM J2.
Pool each channel with its own run count, retaining numerator/denominator
and cross-observable correlations. Predictions are not used for allocation.
The variance forecast assumes comparable independent-run scatter and is not
a guarantee against new subtraction outliers. After this final extension,
report the measured precision including any remaining misses and proceed to
the independent comparison; do not extend the campaign indefinitely.
