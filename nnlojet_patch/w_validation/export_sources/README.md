# Original exported readers and plotters

These are the exact study-export sources, retained for provenance. Their original
hashes appear in `examples/data/w_joint/PRODUCTION_ARTIFACT_SHA256.txt`. The public
entry points with repository-relative defaults and separate output directories
are `examples/w_joint_inputs.py`, `examples/plot_w_joint.py` and
`examples/plot_w_joint_map.py`. Their numerical inputs and comparison definitions
are unchanged. The public map also spells its percent label for matplotlib's
standard mathtext renderer.
